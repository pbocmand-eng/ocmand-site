How we work

An organized, thorough approach to every project, from preconstruction through closeout.

Managed carefully from start to finish.

A successful project depends on how well the details are managed. Ocmand Construction plans each project carefully, manages it closely and stays involved until the work is complete.

The services below are part of every project we build. Each one is handled thoroughly, so nothing is overlooked, the work is done correctly and the schedule is met.

01

Preconstruction

We establish the scope, the budget and the schedule before construction begins, so the project starts with a clear plan.

  • Estimating and budgeting
  • Value engineering
  • Project scheduling
  • Plan and specification review
  • Subcontractor bidding
  • Permitting

02

Project management

We keep the documentation, the materials and the trades organized and ahead of the work.

  • Submittals
  • Requests for information
  • Material procurement
  • Subcontracts and insurance
  • Schedule updates
  • Change orders
  • Pay applications
  • Progress meetings and reporting

03

Construction

Our superintendent directs the work on site every day and holds every trade to the plans, the specifications and the schedule.

  • On-site supervision
  • Subcontractor coordination
  • Quality control
  • Job site safety
  • Inspections and testing
  • Daily reports

04

Closeout

We finish the details, complete the final inspections and turn the building over with the documents the owner needs to operate it.

  • Punch list completion
  • Final inspections
  • Operation and maintenance manuals
  • Warranties
  • Record documents
  • Owner training

Thorough on every project.

We give each of these steps our full attention and follow it through to completion. That is how we make sure nothing is missed, the work is done right and the schedule is met.

Quality

The work is reviewed at every stage against the plans, the specifications and our own standards.

Schedule

The schedule is planned in detail, updated regularly and managed through the last day of the project.

Safety

We maintain a safe, clean and orderly job site for our crews, our subcontractors and the public.

Communication

Owners and architects receive regular updates and prompt answers throughout the project.
