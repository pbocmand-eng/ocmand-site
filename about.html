About the company

A family-owned general contractor with more than 35 years of construction experience.

Family owned. Built on experience.

Ocmand Construction Co., LLC is a family-owned commercial general contractor based in Louisiana. The company is led by Shawn Ocmand, who has more than 35 years of experience building restaurants, offices, medical buildings and public projects.

We serve private owners, developers and public agencies, and we approach every project the same way. We plan it carefully, build it correctly and stand behind the work.

Our reputation is built on dependable work and lasting relationships with the owners, architects and subcontractors we work with.

Our values
