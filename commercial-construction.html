Ground-up commercial construction

New commercial buildings, delivered complete from site work through final completion.

New construction, start to finish.

Ocmand Construction builds new commercial facilities for owners and developers, including office, medical, retail and multi-tenant buildings. We manage every phase of the work under a single contract, from site preparation through the certificate of occupancy.

Our team works closely with the owner and the design team throughout the project to protect the budget, maintain the schedule and deliver a building that meets the drawings and the owner's expectations.

Project types

  • Office buildings
  • Medical and dental offices
  • Retail centers
  • Restaurants
  • Multi-tenant shell buildings
  • Pre-engineered metal buildings
  • Site development and parking
  • Interior build-outs

Our approach

Planning

Careful planning before construction begins, so the budget and the schedule are established early.

Quality

The work is reviewed at every stage to confirm it meets the plans, the specifications and our own standards.

Communication

Owners and architects are kept informed throughout the project, with one point of contact from start to finish.
