Concrete and site work

Foundations, slabs, paving, drainage and site utilities for commercial projects.

The groundwork for every project.

Every building depends on the work beneath it. Ocmand Construction provides concrete and site work for our own building projects and as stand-alone contracts.

We pay close attention to layout, grades and placement, and we verify the work before it is covered, so it performs for the life of the building.

Scope of work

  • Building foundations
  • Slabs on grade
  • Parking lots and paving
  • Sidewalks, curbs and ramps
  • Storm drainage
  • Site utilities
  • Grading and earthwork
  • Erosion control

Our approach

Accuracy

Layout and elevations are checked before any concrete is placed.

Quality

Materials and workmanship are inspected and tested to meet the requirements of the project.

Scheduling

Site work is sequenced so the rest of the project can proceed without delay.
