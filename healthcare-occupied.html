Healthcare and occupied facilities

Renovations in hospitals, clinics and businesses that remain open during construction.

Construction in active facilities.

Renovating a facility that remains in operation requires careful planning. Ocmand Construction works with administrators and facility staff to plan the work, protect patients, staff and the public, and keep daily operations running.

We respect the environment we are working in. Our crews and subcontractors follow the requirements of the facility, keep work areas clean and contained, and schedule disruptive work around the people who use the building.

Project types

  • Hospital and clinic renovations
  • Medical office build-outs
  • Imaging and treatment rooms
  • Occupied office renovations
  • Phased renovations
  • Additions to existing facilities

Our approach

Planning

Phasing, access and utility shutdowns are planned with the facility before work begins.

Safety and cleanliness

Work areas are separated from occupied areas and kept clean, safe and secure.

Coordination

We work around the schedule of the facility and keep its staff informed at every step.
