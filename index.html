About Ocmand Construction

Experience you can build on.

Led by Shawn Ocmand, with 36 years in the trade, Ocmand Construction is a family-run business that has spent decades doing a little of everything.

From custom home building and major residential renovations to restaurants, retail build-outs, and commercial projects of all kinds, we bring the same hands-on standard to every job. As a family-run business, we manage every trade and every detail that turns a plan into a finished space.

The result is a single contractor you can hand the whole project to — residential or commercial — and trust to get it done right.
