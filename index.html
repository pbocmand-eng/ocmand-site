Committed to doing the job right.

Ocmand Construction is a family-owned general contractor led by Shawn Ocmand, who brings more than 35 years of construction experience to every project. We work with owners, architects and public agencies to deliver commercial buildings that are built correctly, finished on schedule and made to last.

We take pride in our work and we stand behind it. Every project receives the same care and attention, whether it is a new building, a renovation or a public contract.

About the company

Preconstruction

Estimating, budgeting, value engineering and scheduling that give owners a clear picture of cost and time before construction begins.

General contracting

Complete responsibility for the project under a single contract, from site work through final completion.

Project management

Close coordination of subcontractors, schedules and documentation to keep every project organized and on track.
