About Ocmand

Experience you can build on.

Led by Shawn Ocmand, with 36 years in the trade, Ocmand Construction has spent decades doing a little of everything.

From custom home building and major residential renovations to restaurants, retail build-outs, and commercial projects of all kinds, we bring the same hands-on standard to every job. We self-perform concrete and sitework and manage the trades that turn a plan into a finished space.

The result is a single contractor you can hand the whole project to — residential or commercial — and trust to get it done right.
