Pre-engineered metal buildings

Metal building systems for commercial use, delivered complete from foundation to finish.

Complete metal building projects.

Pre-engineered metal buildings are an efficient and economical way to build commercial space. Ocmand Construction provides the complete project, including the foundation, the building, and the interior and exterior finishes.

We work with established building manufacturers and experienced erectors, and we coordinate the building with every other part of the project so the work fits together as designed.

Project types

  • Retail and restaurant buildings
  • Office and warehouse buildings
  • Shop and service buildings
  • Storage and equipment buildings
  • Building additions
  • Interior build-outs

Our approach

Coordination

The building system is coordinated with the foundation, the structure and the finishes before fabrication.

Quality

Materials and installation are checked throughout construction to make sure the building is erected properly.

Value

Competitive pricing from building manufacturers gives owners strong value for their budget.
