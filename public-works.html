Public works

Construction for parish, municipal and state agencies.

Building for public owners.

Ocmand Construction bids and builds public projects for parish, municipal and state agencies in Louisiana. We are licensed, bonded and insured, and we are familiar with the requirements of public contracts.

Public owners expect accountability. We follow the contract documents closely, maintain complete project records and deliver the work as specified.

Project types

  • Public buildings and renovations
  • Healthcare facilities
  • Parks and recreation facilities
  • Memorials and site improvements
  • Concrete and site work
  • Additions to existing facilities

Our approach

Compliance

The work is performed in accordance with the contract documents and Louisiana public bid law.

Documentation

Submittals, pay applications and closeout documents are prepared thoroughly and on time.

Accountability

One team is responsible for the project from the bid through final completion.
